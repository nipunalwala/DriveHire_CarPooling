# 13 · Authentication email and deferred notifications

Product notifications, recipient feeds, unread counts, read controls, booking/seat email and channel preferences are deferred. Do not create notification tables/endpoints/screens or enqueue unhandled product events. Booking/provider decisions and participation state are read in their own screens, with explicit refresh and action errors.

Authentication email remains required. Create verification/reset challenge plus AUTH_VERIFY_EMAIL or AUTH_RESET_PASSWORD outbox row atomically. Store only safe IDs in payload; raw OTP/reset secret is authenticated-encrypted with a private configured key. Worker claims jobs with SKIP LOCKED lease, decrypts outside transaction, sends through real SMTP, then records completion only after provider acceptance. Retry with bounded exponential backoff and safe error codes; expiry/dead-letter leaves an honest failed/pending challenge with resend/recovery. At-least-once delivery may duplicate email; challenge use is one-time and fixed expiry. Never log bodies/secrets or claim provider acceptance means mailbox receipt. Test crash/retry, Mailpit message contents, duplicate delivery/token replay and unavailable SMTP.
